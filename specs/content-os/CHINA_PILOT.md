# China Content OS pilot

Language: EN first. Publishing: human approval. Autonomous posting: out of scope.

## Method

`content -> WS focus/dimensions -> ICP -> trigger -> goal/outcome -> Ranking v1 -> China programme/pathway -> lead -> booking -> performance feedback`

WS is not merely a CTA. It is the shared user-state layer between acquisition content and programme matching.

## Initial briefs

### Executive burnout -> Wudang reset
- WS dimensions: recovery, mind
- Trigger: problem-aware / destination-led
- Programme: `wudang-executive-burnout-recovery`
- CTA stage: assessment / Get Wellness Score
- Evidence state: hypothesis until programme/property facts and proof are retrieved.

### Sleep & mind reset -> Wudang
- WS focus: Sleep & Recovery
- WS dimensions: recovery, mind
- Trigger: problem-aware / experience-led
- Programme: `wudang-5-day-dao-wellness`
- CTA stage: assessment / Get Wellness Score
- No medical claims.

### Preventive longevity -> Mile
- WS focus: Longevity
- WS dimensions: body, recovery, metabolic, risk
- Trigger: outcome-aware / treatment-aware
- Programme: `mile-executive-health-recovery`
- CTA stage: consideration / Check-up
- `medical_wellness`: mandatory human approval and source-grounded claims.

### Medical China -> coordinated clinical pathway
- WS dimension: risk
- Trigger: treatment-aware / destination-led
- Programme: `bm-package-a-outpatient-fast-track`
- CTA stage: medical / Submit Your Case
- `medical`: no diagnosis, no wellness outcome claims, no price-led comparison, mandatory human approval.

## Retrieval gate before generation

For every brief retrieve the programme, linked property/destination, duration, price/inclusions where relevant, services/modalities, goal/outcome mappings, ICP/triggers, reviews/proof, approved media and medical level. Copy generation may use only retrieved facts and must preserve `confirmed / hypothesis / missing` status.

## Measurement

Content performance should eventually resolve to the same journey graph: content/UTM -> WS assessment -> `journey_search` -> `journey_match` -> lead -> booking -> gross margin. Aggregate WS dimensions also become an input to the Strategy Agent for future content allocation.
